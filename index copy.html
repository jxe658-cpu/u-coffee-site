Why we stop at 200 mg

The FDA cites about 400 mg a day as an amount generally not linked to negative effects for healthy adults. So even our strongest can, U4, leaves room in your day. U Coffee is about the right energy, not the most.

The app keeps count

Log each can in the U Coffee app and a meter shows your total for the day. Pick a high level late at night and the app suggests U0 instead, so your bedtime stays yours.

Our Story

Three UM students who were tired of one-size-fits-all coffee.

We kept seeing the same thing: students buying a strong coffee at 9 PM, lying awake until 2 AM, then needing an even stronger one for their 8 AM. Others skipped coffee completely because one cup made them jittery.

So we asked a different question. Instead of how you take your coffee, how awake do you want to be? U Coffee is our answer, built at the University of Miami for the way students actually live.
